Hiring pipeline — revised components map

The session map in Trigger → Input → Context → Processing → AI → Output form, redrawn with the three steps the nine checks force into existence: the privacy boundary, the rank computation that decides invite from rejection, and the confirm gate that is the only edge out.

What changed, and why it had to

Added · System

Privacy boundary

The original map had "excludes personal details from AI" as a parenthetical inside the extraction box. It is not a parenthetical. It is the one step that decides whether this is DPDP-defensible, and it is the one step that must not be an AI step — if a model does the separating, the details have already left the building before they were separated.

Added · System

Rank & line

Invite or rejection is not a property of a candidate. It is a property of their rank, and rank changes every time another CV is added. Drawing it as its own box makes it clear the email type has to be recomputed, not decided once at generation time.

Added · Founder

Confirm gate

The Cut lives here. Drawing it as a box in the founder lane makes visible what the prose only asserts: the only edge into Resend originates in a human action, on one named candidate, one at a time.

The Cut

Asked for: auto-send to rejected and selected candidates once he confirms. Killed by: check 06 and check 09.

Automated: extraction, PII separation, scoring, ranking, brief generation, email drafting. Human: the shortlist review, the confirm-to-send decision, the hiring call.

The system ranks and explains. Arjun looks at everything below the line once — ten minutes, not ten hours, because the ranking does the heavy lifting. Nothing is rejected on his behalf, because a wrong rejection is one he cannot fix.
